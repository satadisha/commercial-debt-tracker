
Project Overview 💻

This repository contains machine learning research conducted in collaboration with BankTrack, an international NGO focused on tracking the role of private sector commercial banks in financing activities with potential environmental and human rights impacts.

The project develops and evaluates a text classification system to identify whether U.S. SEC Form 8-K filings contain information relevant to commercial debt relationships, enabling advocacy organizations to surface financial ties that are often opaque or underreported.
